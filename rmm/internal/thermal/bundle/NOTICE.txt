licensed under Mozilla Public License 2.0.
Source corresponding to this release:
https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor/tree/3d331e3370efb858411f19511373eff65a218701
LibreHardwareMonitor copyright and third-party notices are in LICENSE.txt and
upstream-notices.txt. NuGet dependency licenses are in third-party-notices.txt.
Only read access to temperature sensors is used.
PawnIO 2.1.0 is bundled from the same upstream release. Its SHA-256 is:
a3a46226c5e2824f4cdd42be0eecbabfc672c86f7889710f5ab1e6ad385b47a0
The Windows agent installs it silently at startup if 2.1.0 or newer is absent.
PawnIO-COPYING.txt contains the driver license. Corresponding source archives
accompany the unmodified driver and its embedded LGPL modules; extract PawnPP
into the PawnIO source's PawnPP subdirectory to reconstruct the submodule.
