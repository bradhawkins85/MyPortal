MyPortal tray Windows release builds include, in this folder, the files the
AI health check uses to read hardware sensors. They are added at build time by
tray/sensor-bundle/build.py and are not kept in source control:

- LibreHardwareMonitorLib 0.9.6 (unmodified, Mozilla Public License 2.0) and
  its NuGet dependencies. Source:
  https://github.com/LibreHardwareMonitor/LibreHardwareMonitor/tree/3d331e3370efb858411f19511373eff65a218701
  Licences and notices: LICENSE.txt, upstream-notices.txt and
  third-party-notices.txt.
- The PawnIO 2.1.0 driver installer (PawnIO_setup.exe, unmodified), SHA-256
  a3a46226c5e2824f4cdd42be0eecbabfc672c86f7889710f5ab1e6ad385b47a0.
  PawnIO-COPYING.txt holds the driver licence. Corresponding source archives
  accompany the driver and its embedded LGPL modules (extract PawnPP into the
  PawnIO source's PawnPP subdirectory to reconstruct the submodule):
  PawnIO: https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO/tree/e1043d7750f501b6b0d3ac0a71bd5b9406cd17b6
  PawnPP: https://github.com/namazso/PawnPP/tree/e64e4c37b2d8ba0d8ee57205faf8183aee12c438
  Modules: https://github.com/namazso/PawnIO.Modules/tree/4aa792beb020a14c8261072e9786d2dfb38489d9

The library only reads sensors. The health check installs PawnIO on physical
Windows hardware for the length of one check, when the portal allows it, and
uninstalls it again before reporting back.
