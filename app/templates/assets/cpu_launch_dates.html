    How device age is estimated

    A computer cannot be older than its processor, so each device's Approx age is the number of years since its CPU was launched. Record each processor's launch date once and every device with it is updated. For Intel processors, choose Open on Intel ARK, find the Launch Date under Supplemental Information (for example Q3'19) and type it in as shown.
