Blocked Windows updates

MyPortal never installs a blocked update: patch policies skip it and technicians cannot choose it on a device. Devices hide blocked updates from Windows Update at their next daily scan, so automatic updates leave them alone, and show them again after you unblock. Block an update for one device, this company or every company from the device's asset page, or block a KB number here. Blocks for every company need a super admin.
