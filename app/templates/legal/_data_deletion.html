Account anonymisation (the right to be forgotten)

You can ask us to anonymise your account, permanently removing your personal information from the portal. To request this, use the "Danger zone" at the bottom of your profile in {{ app_name }}, or {{ dd_contact }}. Before we action the request we will verify who you are, and we can refuse or defer it where the law requires us to keep the information (for example for tax, accounting, legal or security obligations).

When your request is processed we will, among other things:

  • replace your name with a generic placeholder and change your email address to an undeliverable one, so your account can no longer be identified from them;
  • delete your password, two-factor authentication, passkey and mobile phone details, and deactivate the account so it can no longer be signed in to;
  • remove or de-identify the personal information in your tickets, replies, chats, call and SMS records and attachments;
  • delete any artificial-intelligence (AI) documents that were created from your information; and
  • remove you from sales and marketing communications.

This is permanent and cannot be undone, so please save any information you need before you ask. Because our backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, they stop containing your personal information over time. We may keep a small amount of information where we are required to by law. We also keep a record that your account was anonymised so that we can honour your request and meet our compliance obligations; that record does not include your name or email address — it references your account using an irreversible, one-way code that cannot be turned back into your details.
