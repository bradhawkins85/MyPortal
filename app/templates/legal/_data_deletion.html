Deleting or anonymising your account

{% if current_user %} You can ask us to delete or anonymise your account using the Delete or anonymise my account form on your profile. {% else %} You can ask us to delete or anonymise your account. Once you've signed in, use the "Delete or anonymise my account" form on your profile. {% endif %} You can also email {{ dd_contact }}. We'll confirm who you are before we act, and we'll email you when your request has been approved or, with a reason, if we can't action it.

Some records have to be kept for business, legal or tax reasons, such as invoices, orders and support tickets. We don't delete those records. Instead we anonymise them by removing your personal details. When your request is approved we:

  • replace your name with "Anonymised user" and your email address with an address that can't receive email;
  • remove your phone numbers and email signature, delete your password, two-factor authentication and passkeys, sign you out everywhere, and close the account so it can't be signed in to;
  • remove your name, email address and phone number from your tickets, replies, chats, SMS and call records, and from staff records linked to you;
  • delete your call recordings, voicemail and their transcripts, and email open and click tracking;
  • remove your information from our artificial intelligence (AI) search index; and
  • stop all sales and marketing emails to you.

This can't be undone, so save anything you need before you ask. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis, so they stop holding your personal information over time. To keep you off future marketing lists, we keep a one-way code made from your email address that can't be turned back into the address. Our security records note that an account was anonymised but don't hold your name or email address.
