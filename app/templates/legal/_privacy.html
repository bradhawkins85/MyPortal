1. Who we are

{{ app_name }} is a customer support and service portal operated by {{ legal_entity_name }} ("we", "us", "our"). We use it to provide IT support, manage the services and products we supply to your organisation, and keep you informed. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through the portal, its desktop tray app, its email, chat, phone and SMS channels, and the services connected to it.

In most cases your organisation (our customer) decides which of its people and systems we look after, and we handle information about you on its behalf. Where we do, your organisation's own privacy policy also applies. We aim to comply with applicable privacy laws, including the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth) and, where it applies, the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

2. Information we collect

Depending on which features your organisation uses, we may collect:

Account and contact details
Your name, email address, mobile phone number, job title, the companies you belong to and your role and permissions in each.
Sign-in and security information
A one-way (bcrypt) hash of your password — never the password itself — your encrypted authenticator (two-factor) secret, the public key of any passkeys you register, email verification and password reset tokens, and the date and time of your sign-ins.
Usage, device and log information
Your IP address, browser and device type (user agent), the pages and actions you use, and an audit trail of changes made in the portal, including changes made by our staff while acting on your account. We use this for security, fraud prevention and troubleshooting.
Support requests and communications
The tickets you raise and their replies, attachments and history; emails sent to our support mailboxes; chat conversations; time recorded against your requests; and form submissions.
Calls, voicemail and SMS
If our phone and SMS services are enabled, call details (numbers, times and durations), call recordings and voicemail, automatic transcripts of those recordings, and the content of SMS messages sent to or from us.
Staff and organisation records
Information your organisation gives us about its staff so we can manage their accounts and access, such as names, contact details, departments, managers, start and finish dates and onboarding or offboarding details.
Devices, assets and networks we manage
Device names, serial numbers, hardware and software inventory, the signed-in user, operating system and security status, network addresses (IP and MAC), photos of equipment, and information from our remote monitoring and management, endpoint security, uptime and website monitoring tools. The {{ app_name }} tray app installed on managed computers reports device details so we can match the computer to your organisation's assets and let you raise tickets and chat with us.
Microsoft 365 and email security information
Where your organisation connects its Microsoft 365 tenant, directory information about users and mailboxes, licences, sign-in and security settings, email signatures, out-of-office settings, emails reported as suspicious, and DMARC reports about email sent using your organisation's domains.
Orders, billing and subscriptions
Products, quotes, orders, subscriptions, licences, invoices, delivery details and billing contacts. If card payments are offered they are processed by the payment provider; {{ app_name }} does not store full card numbers.
Credentials and documentation you store with us
Passwords, business continuity plans, compliance evidence, processes and other documentation you or your organisation choose to keep in the portal. Stored credentials are encrypted.
AI assistant questions and outputs
Questions you ask the AI assistant, searches you save, and AI-generated content such as ticket summaries, tags, suggested replies and call transcripts (see section 4).
Marketing and email engagement
If you receive our newsletters, campaign emails, or emails about your tickets and replies, whether you opened them or clicked a link (using a tracking image and tracked links). For ticket-related emails, we record the date and time of the open or click, your IP address, and your browser and device details (user agent). We also record your unsubscribe preferences for marketing emails.

Most of this information comes from you, your organisation, or the systems your organisation asks us to manage. You do not have to give us personal information, but without it we may not be able to provide support or create an account for you.

3. How we use it

We use personal information to:

  • create and secure your account, verify your identity and keep the portal safe from misuse;
  • receive, route, answer and track your support requests, chats, calls and messages;
  • manage, monitor and protect the devices, accounts, networks and services we look after for your organisation;
  • process quotes, orders, subscriptions, invoices and payments, and keep business records;
  • send you service notifications, ticket updates and, where permitted, newsletters (which you can unsubscribe from);
  • confirm that support updates about your tickets have been received by seeing whether a notification email was opened or a link was clicked;
  • produce reports for your organisation, measure service levels and improve our services; and
  • meet our legal, tax, audit and insurance obligations and resolve disputes.

Where the GDPR applies, we rely on performing our contract with you or your organisation, our legitimate interests in running a secure and effective support service, compliance with legal obligations and, for marketing, your consent.

4. Artificial intelligence (AI) features

{{ app_name }} includes optional features that use artificial intelligence, including large language models and speech recognition. Depending on what is enabled, AI may be used to:

  • answer questions you ask the portal's AI assistant or search, using only information your account is allowed to see;
  • suggest knowledge base articles and similar resolved tickets while you raise a ticket;
  • summarise, title, tag, categorise and prioritise tickets, spot likely duplicates and related records, and draft follow-up questions or resolution steps for our staff;
  • suggest replies that our staff review before sending;
  • respond to you in chat while you wait for a technician (you will be talking to an automated assistant, and you can ask for a person at any time);
  • transcribe call recordings and voicemail; and
  • let our staff look up tickets from approved AI tools through secure connectors.

What information is used. These features process the content you give us — ticket and chat text, attachments' extracted text, call audio and transcripts, and knowledge base content — together with related account, company and asset details. To make search and suggestions work, this content is also turned into a searchable index (including numerical "embeddings"). The index follows the same permissions as the portal, so it never shows you information you could not otherwise see.

Where it is processed. Where possible, we run AI models on infrastructure we control, so your information does not leave our systems. Some features may use a third-party AI provider (for example OpenAI). When they do, the relevant content is sent to that provider to generate a response and is handled under its terms and privacy policy, which may involve processing outside Australia. Where a provider offers the option, we choose settings that do not allow it to use our customers' information to train its models.

No training on your data by us. We do not use your personal information to train or fine-tune AI models.

Human oversight. AI output assists our team; it can be inaccurate. We do not use AI to make decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you without a person reviewing them. We apply safeguards to keep AI features from following instructions hidden in submitted content and from revealing information to people who should not see it.

Your choices. You can ask to speak to a person instead of the chat assistant, and you can ask us not to process your requests with AI features where practical — contact us using the details in section 11. Some features, such as transcription, may be essential to the service your organisation has chosen.

5. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only as needed to provide our services:

  • Your organisation — administrators at your company can see tickets, staff records, assets and other information relating to their organisation.
  • Service providers who run parts of the service for us, such as hosting, email delivery, SMS and telephony, accounting and invoicing, remote monitoring and management, endpoint security, documentation, project management, time tracking and notification services.
  • AI providers — where an AI feature uses a third-party model rather than one we host ourselves, the content needed to generate the response (see section 4).
  • Microsoft and other platforms your organisation asks us to manage, when we act on those systems on its behalf.
  • Suppliers and couriers when we fulfil an order you place.
  • Professional advisers, insurers and authorities where required by law, to protect our rights or safety, or in connection with a sale or restructure of our business.

Our service providers may only use the information to provide services to us and must protect it.

6. Cookies and browser storage

{{ app_name }} uses only the cookies it needs to work. It does not use advertising or third-party tracking cookies.

  • Session cookie — keeps you signed in. It expires when your session ends or after the maximum session length.
  • Security (CSRF) cookie — protects your forms and actions from being submitted by other websites.
  • Passkey sign-in cookie — briefly links the steps of a passkey sign-in.
  • Message cookie — shows a one-off confirmation message on the next page and expires within a minute.
  • Tray app chat cookie — keeps a chat window opened from the tray app connected for a short time.

Your browser's local storage is also used to remember display preferences such as table columns, collapsed panels and unsent reply drafts. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Email open and click tracking (described in section 2) uses a small tracking image and tracked links embedded in the email itself — it does not rely on cookies. If you block remote images in your email client, the tracking image will not load and we will not be able to record that you opened the email.

7. How we protect it

We take reasonable steps to protect personal information from misuse, interference, loss and unauthorised access, including encrypted connections (HTTPS), hashed passwords, two-factor authentication and passkeys, encryption of stored secrets and credentials, role-based access controls, rate limiting, audit logging and regular backups. When our staff sign in as you to help with a problem, this is recorded in the audit log. No system is perfectly secure; if we become aware of a data breach likely to cause serious harm we will notify you and the relevant regulator as required by law.

8. How long we keep it

We keep personal information for as long as we provide services to you or your organisation and afterwards for as long as needed for legal, tax, accounting and dispute purposes. Audit logs are kept for 12 months by default, application logs are rotated automatically, and DMARC reports and monitoring data are pruned on a schedule. When information is no longer needed we delete or de-identify it. Backups are overwritten on a rolling basis.

  • ask for access to, or correction of, the personal information we hold about you;
  • ask us to delete or restrict use of your information, or object to how we use it, where the law allows;
  • ask for a copy of your information in a portable format where the GDPR applies;
  • unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link in any email (service and security notifications will still be sent); and
  • withdraw consent where we rely on it.

Where we handle your information on behalf of your organisation, we may refer your request to it. To make a request or complaint, contact us by {{ contact }}. We will respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied, you can complain to the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (oaic.gov.au) or your local data protection authority.
