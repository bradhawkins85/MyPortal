The approved list

The approved list is the built-in set of event logs and log folders, plus the TROUBLESHOOT_EVENT_LOGS_WINDOWS and TROUBLESHOOT_LOG_FOLDERS_WINDOWS / _MACOS settings, plus every log approved here. Only files with log extensions (.log, .txt, .wer, .lo_, .ips, .crash, .panic, .diag) are read from an approved folder, and the tray app checks every entry again before reading anything.
