How publishing works
  • Draft templates can be edited freely and aren't used anywhere.
  • Published templates are available to MyPortal. Of those active today (in {{ schedule_timezone }}), the highest priority wins, then the default, then the earliest start date.
  • Disabled templates are kept but never used.
  • Publishing doesn't deploy signatures to Outlook or other Microsoft 365 clients — client deployment isn't configured, and personal or third-party signatures stay outside MyPortal.
