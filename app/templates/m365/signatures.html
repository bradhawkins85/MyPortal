MyPortal signature templates

Manage tenant-scoped HTML and plain-text signature templates, preview them against staff data, and publish or disable them for consumers inside MyPortal. Publishing changes a database lifecycle state; it does not deploy a signature to Outlook or other Microsoft 365 clients.

Lifecycle: Draft templates are editable but inactive. Published means available for MyPortal use. A published template becomes scheduled or active according to its dates in {{ schedule_timezone }}. Client deployment: not configured — this workspace does not synchronize Outlook signatures.

Current platform boundary

This workspace stores and selects templates for MyPortal. It has no supported delivery integration, so actual Outlook deployment status is not configured rather than pending or successful.

  • HTML and TXT variants are stored separately so administrators can publish rich and plain-text versions independently.
  • Preview uses live staff, company, custom-variable, and automation-token data before publication.
  • Existing personal or third-party signatures remain outside MyPortal ownership.
