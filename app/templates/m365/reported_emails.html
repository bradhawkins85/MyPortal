User-reported email alerts

Microsoft Defender alerts raised when a user reports a message as phish, malware or junk. Creating a search runs a non-destructive compliance search for the P1 sender and subject, received one hour either side of the reported message. Nothing is purged until you confirm it on the Spam Search & Purge page.
